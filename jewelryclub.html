Monta Vista Jewelry Making Club

VP of Marketing · Jan 2025 – present

As VP of Marketing for Monta Vista's Jewelry Making Club, I owned the club's entire visual identity — every flyer, business card, event promo, and Instagram post — along with the advertising strategy behind it. The posts I designed reached 20k+ views on the club's Instagram.

Consistent brand system

Brand system

I designed the club's core mark — a monogram badge in a warm pink-to-peach gradient — and carried it consistently across every piece of collateral so the club had a recognizable look no matter where someone encountered it: a flyer on a bulletin board, a business card handed out at a club fair, or a post on Instagram.

Event promotion

Beyond the core brand, I designed graphics for individual events — workshops, grub days, and info sessions — each one built to grab attention scrolling past on Instagram while still feeling like it belonged to the same club. "Club Grub Day" paired the club's palette with actual food photography to promote a Somi Somi taiyaki fundraiser; the Info Day reminder was styled like a phone notification to feel native to how people actually scroll.

Strategy & results

Beyond design, I planned the club's advertising strategy and pushed consistently to increase awareness of club activities — which is what got those posts to 20k+ views. Consistent branding across every material meant the club built real recognition on campus instead of starting from zero with every new post.

Role: VP of Marketing, Monta Vista High School Jewelry Making Club. Designed all promotional materials and led advertising strategy.
