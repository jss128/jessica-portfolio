Nails by Jessi

Founder & nail artist · @nailsbyjessi__ · July 2025 – present

Nails by Jessi is a press-on nail business I run from scratch — designing custom sets, hand-painting the nail art, and building the brand around it, right down to a how-to-apply guide so first-time customers get a clean application at home.

Design & brand

Every set is designed and hand-painted by me, from color story to finish — polka dots, pearl swirls, 3D floral accents, chrome tips. Beyond the nail art itself, I built out the brand identity for the business, including a logo, so the whole experience feels considered from the Instagram grid to the box that ships out.

Customer experience

Since press-ons live or die on the application, I designed a step-by-step "How to Apply" guide — cut nails, wash hands, buff, push back cuticles, then glue at a 45° angle from the base — so customers actually get the result they see in the photos, not a rushed, uneven application.

Find the shop on Instagram at @nailsbyjessi__.
